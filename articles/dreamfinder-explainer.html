You had a brilliant idea this morning. It sparked. It glowed. And then… poof. Where did it go?

Ideas move fast. Notebooks… don’t. And every AI assistant you talk to? It starts from zero. Every. Single. Time.

Meet Dreamfinder. It gives all of your AI assistants one shared memory: your own notes.

When an idea sparks, just send it. Type it. Say it. Snap a photo. Share a link, or a PDF. In the car, on a walk, at midnight. It all lands in the same library. No folder to pick. No tags to invent. It files itself.

Dreamfinder hands every assistant your house rules: where things go, how they’re tagged, how they connect. So you never explain it twice.

Later, you won’t remember the exact words. You don’t need to. Just ask: “what did I decide about the cabin wiring?” And it finds the note by meaning, even if it never uses those words.

Best of all? You don’t have to remember to go looking. Every morning at nine, an old spark drops by to say hello. And twice a day, the inbox tidies itself, while a quiet checkup catches stray notes and broken links, before they pile up.

Step away for a week. Or a month. Nothing’s lost. Every change is recorded: who, when, and what it looked like before. So anything can be undone. And if a new note contradicts an old one, it gets flagged, not overwritten.

Your notes stay plain text, in a folder you own. Open them. Edit them by hand. Take them anywhere. Unplug Dreamfinder tomorrow, and every word is still yours.

It’s named for a traveler who wanders off… and comes back with something worth keeping. Dreamfinder. Catch the spark. Keep the thread.
